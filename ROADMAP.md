# metaprev roadmap

## Now

1. Maintain the 0.5.0 repair workspace and its security, fallback, crop, and exit-code contracts.
2. Keep `@forsvn/metaprev`, the bundled skill, and the public mirror aligned.

## Next

Create a lightweight feedback path from real package use, then use that evidence to choose the
next compatibility fixes.

## Later

A dedicated brand or landing page is optional and should follow sustained demand. Hosted scanning,
analytics, and generic SEO advice are not default scope.
